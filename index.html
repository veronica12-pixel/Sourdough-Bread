How to use

A simple guide for this bake timer.

1. Choose a recipe

Tap Select Recipe and pick Sourdough Bread, Pizza, Focaccia, Cioccolatella, or a custom recipe. Built-in recipes load ingredients and bake settings for you. Use Qty to scale the batch.

2. Work through the phases

Tap a phase (Levain, Autolyse, Bulk, and so on) to open it. There's no running clock — when you finish that step in real life, tap Mark Done and it logs the time for you. Tap it again to un-mark it if you tapped by mistake. Use Skip this step if you do not need it.

Each Done note shows how much time passed since the previous step, calculated automatically from the timestamps — no need to watch a clock.

3. Folds

Open Folds during bulk. Tap + Log a fold each time you do a stretch or coil fold — it logs the count and the time. For Cioccolatella, add chocolate on folds 2–3 when reminded. Close the Folds panel yourself when you are done.

4. When a timer finishes

You get a sound, optional vibration, and a large bread screen. Tap the bread to dismiss. For most steps that also closes the phase; Folds and Covered Bake stay open so you can continue.

5. Flour used, Temp & PH

On Levain, Autolyse, and Bulk you can type which flour you used, plus Temp and PH for that stage. On Levain, you can also note which starter you used. Pre-Shape, Bench Rest, and Cold Proof each have their own Temp and PH fields too. All of these notes are saved with the bake.

6. Progress & chart

Completed steps appear under Progress. A vertical bar chart shows how long each step took.

7. Bake

Bread-style bakes use Score, then Covered Bake and Uncovered Bake. With a Dutch oven, Covered is lid on; Uncovered is lid off. Pizza and Focaccia use one timed bake with their preset temperature and minutes.

8. End the session

Tap End Session. Review the recap, then Save to Bake Log or Discard this bake.

9. Bake Log & Save

BAKE LOG shows past bakes. SAVE backs up the app, exports data, shares a read-only copy, or enables notifications.

10. Extra tools

Loaf / Pizza / Focaccia weight and Baker’s % help scale and check hydration. Export/Import recipes backs up custom recipes. Closing mid-bake may offer Resume next time.

Tip: On iPhone, Add to Home Screen for the best full-screen experience.
